# Third-party sources and distribution notes

- ESM-2: https://github.com/facebookresearch/esm, MIT license. The final pipeline reads frozen model outputs; the model name, extraction parameters, indices, recovered sequence inputs and checkpoint digest are documented in the model card and `data/provenance/esm2/`.
- scFoundation: https://github.com/biomap-research/scFoundation. Source code is Apache-2.0; model weights use the separate non-commercial research `MODEL_LICENSE`. Both notices accompany the vendored implementation. The B2 checkpoint is derived from this backbone and retains applicable upstream restrictions.
- UniProt/SGD protein and identifier data, orthology, GO annotations and expression datasets retain the terms and attribution listed in `data/THIRD_PARTY_DATA.md`. Exact content versions, use, source and acquisition evidence are recorded in `data/datasets.json`.
- scGPT, Geneformer and scYeast are used in model comparisons. The extended archive contains frozen comparison representations. Its scYeast gene table is the unchanged `net.pos_embedding.emb.weight` tensor extracted from the archived checkpoint, with the upstream [MIT notice](models/comparisons/scyeast/LICENSE), source attribution and tensor hashes. Full scYeast, scGPT and Geneformer pretrained checkpoints are not redistributed by this extended release.
- Costanzo et al. (2016), DOI 10.1126/science.aaf1420: the registered graph comparator uses a derived SGA table from the [official dataset](https://thecellmap.org/yeast/costanzo2016/). The graph archive includes original-input hashes, the strain-to-ORF transformation and source attribution. It retains the authors' data rights.

This repository does not assign a new project-wide license or replace third-party terms. The core inference archive contains the submitted B2 checkpoint and applicable model notice. The earlier competition review and full bundles retain their original contents. Numerical results retain the scope and limitations stated in the model card.
